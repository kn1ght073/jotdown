jotdown

A tiny single-file notes app. Notes live in your browser; snapshots are AES-GCM encrypted.

    Encrypted snapshots

    Export your notes as an encrypted snapshot, or open one (bundled or from disk). Snapshots can carry notes or arbitrary files; the payload is sealed with AES-256-GCM and the key is derived from your passphrase with PBKDF2 (600,000 iterations). The passphrase never leaves this page and is never stored.
